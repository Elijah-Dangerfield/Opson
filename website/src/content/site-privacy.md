## This website and the waitlist

This part covers the website you're on. The rest of the page is the privacy
policy for the Opson app.

**No cookies or trackers.** The site has no cookies, no analytics and no
third-party scripts. Fonts and images come from the site itself. It's hosted
on Cloudflare Pages, which sees your IP address to serve the page and keeps
the usual server logs; see
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

**What we keep if you join the waitlist.** Your email address, when you added
it, and which page or link you signed up from. The form sends these to our
server on Fly.io, which stores them in our database at Supabase. The server
also looks at your IP address to limit how often the form can be sent, and
doesn't store it.

**What it's for.** One email when Opson can be downloaded, and maybe a few
product updates before then. Nothing else. Your address is never sold or
shared.

**Getting off the list.** Reply to any of those emails, or write to
[contact@nightjarlabs.llc](mailto:contact@nightjarlabs.llc), and we'll delete
your address. Otherwise we keep it until we stop running the waitlist.
