## Food data

Nutrition values come from public food databases and from the people who add
to them. They are estimates and can be wrong.

- **USDA FoodData Central.** U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural
  Research Service. FoodData Central. [fdc.nal.usda.gov](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/).
  Public domain (CC0 1.0).
- **Open Food Facts.** Contains data from
  [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/), available under the
  [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
  Individual contents of the database are available under the
  [Database Contents License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/).
