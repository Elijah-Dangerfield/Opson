This policy is for people in Washington, Nevada and Connecticut, whose laws
give consumer health data its own protections. It covers the Opson app made by
Nightjar Labs LLC. It takes effect on October 27, 2026. Our
[Privacy Policy](https://nightjarlabs.llc/opson/privacy) covers everything
else.

## The consumer health data we collect, and why

| What | Why we collect it and how we use it |
|---|---|
| Your food log: foods, amounts, nutrition values, meal times, water and fasts | To show your log, totals and trends, and to sync them between your phones |
| Weigh-ins | To show them and your trend, if you add them |
| How meals and days felt: hunger, fullness, mood, notes and check-ins | To show them back to you alongside your log |
| Your setup answers, including whether numbers or tracking have been hard for you before | To choose gentler starting settings for you; nothing else |
| Targets and the numbers you choose to see | To apply your settings everywhere in the app |
| What you search for and type to find foods | To find matching foods. Searches are kept without any link to you; typed meals are not kept |
| Meal photos, if you use photo estimates | To list the foods in the photo. Not kept |
| Photos of a pack and the food's details, if you add a food from its pack | To read the label, and so we can check the food before adding it to the database everyone searches. The photos are seen only by you and our reviewer and deleted 30 days after review; an approved food's details are shared with everyone, without the photos or any link to you |
| Energy, steps and workouts from Apple Health or Health Connect, if you connect them | To show them in the app. Read on your phone only; never sent to us |
| Weigh-ins, height, birth year and sex copied from Apple Health or Health Connect, only if you turn on the import or tap Fill in | To keep your log and your energy estimates up to date. Saved and synced like the same details typed in |
| Medication, doses, injection sites and side effects, if you track a medication | To show them back to you, and in the provider summary if you include them. Kept on your phone only; never sent to us |

Totals, trends and patterns the app works out from these are consumer health
data too, and we use them the same way.

## Where it comes from

From you, when you use the app, and from Apple Health or Health Connect on your
phone if you give the app permission. We don't buy or receive consumer health
data from anyone else.

## Who we share it with

We share consumer health data only with the processors that run the app for
us, under contracts that let them use it only for that:

- **Hosting and storage**: Fly.io and Supabase, which run our server and store
  your synced log.
- **Review help**: Anthropic, whose AI assistant the person reviewing a food
  you submitted may use to check its photos against its numbers. It gets the
  photos and the food's details, never your account or your log.
- **Crash reporting**: Sentry. Crash reports are built to leave out your log,
  but a report about a failed food search can include the words searched.

We have no affiliates that receive it. We don't sell consumer health data, and
we don't share it with anyone for their own purposes. We don't use geofencing.

## Consent

We collect consumer health data only as needed to provide the features you ask
for. If we ever want to collect or share it for another purpose, we will ask
for your consent first, separately, and you can withdraw it at any time.

## Your rights, and how to use them

You can:

- confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get
  a copy of it, with a list of every third party and affiliate we've shared it
  with and how to contact them;
- delete it, which we pass on to our processors;
- withdraw any consent you've given;
- appeal if we turn down a request.

Use **More → Support and sharing** in the app to export or delete your data, or
email [contact@nightjarlabs.llc](mailto:contact@nightjarlabs.llc) for anything
else. We answer within 45 days. If we need longer, we'll tell you within those
45 days, and take no more than 45 more. Requests are free up to twice a year.
Because most Opson accounts have no name or email attached, we may ask you for
details from the app so we can find yours.

Deleting your account removes your data from our database straight away and
from backups within 30 days.

To appeal, reply to our answer and say you're appealing. We decide within 45
days and explain our decision. If you disagree with it, you can contact the
Washington Attorney General at [atg.wa.gov](https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint),
the Nevada Attorney General at [ag.nv.gov](https://ag.nv.gov/Complaints/File_Complaint/),
or the Connecticut Attorney General at [portal.ct.gov/ag](https://portal.ct.gov/ag).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. If a
change is material, we will tell you in the app before it applies to you, and
ask for consent where the law requires it.
