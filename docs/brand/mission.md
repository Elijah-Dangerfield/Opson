# Opson: mission and beliefs

## Mission statement

Pick one. The first is the recommendation.

1. **Opson is a food log that fits the person using it. You decide how many numbers you see, and a hard day never costs you the next one.**
2. **Log what you eat with all the numbers, some of them, or none, and never feel worse for opening the app.**
3. **A food tracker for every relationship with food, including the complicated ones.**

## "Open doors to everyone," rephrased

The idea is that nobody should be turned away: not the person who wants every
gram, and not the person who can't look at a calorie today. Options:

- **Room for everyone at the table.** Warm, food-shaped, easy to say out loud.
- **However you eat, there's a way in.** Plainer, and puts the person first.
- **Every way of tracking is a real way of tracking.** Better as a belief than
  a tagline. It says the No numbers user isn't on a lesser version of the app.

## What we believe

### You choose how many numbers you see

Numbers help some people and hurt others, and the same person can need
different things in different years. Opson has four modes (All numbers, Macros
with grams, Macros only, No numbers), and the choice holds on every screen.
Every new screen gets a leak test, because a hidden calorie that shows up while
you add a food breaks the promise.

### A goal is a range, not a line to fall off

A single target turns every day into pass or fail. Opson sets goals as a range
by default, and a bar that reaches the top of it just reads as full. There is
no red, no "over", and no negative number left for the day.

### A big day is just a day

The worst part of a big day is often the plan to make up for it tomorrow. When
you wrap up a big day, Opson says "Everyone has big days. You don't need to make
up for today," and explains that going back to your usual range works better
than eating less to compensate.

### You can step away without starting over

Some days you won't want to track: a holiday, a funeral, a week that's already
too much. Any day can be marked untracked. Trends skips it, and your run of
days waits for you instead of breaking.

### We celebrate showing up, never eating less

Celebrations count the days you logged anything, checked in or wrapped up the
day. They never read an amount, a goal or a deficit, so there is no way to earn
one by eating less. One quiet day is forgiven, they are gentle by default for
anyone who said numbers have been hard, and "I'd rather not see these" turns
them off.

### Safety notes point to people

When something looks risky, Opson says so plainly and points to help rather
than locking you out. A calorie goal below what most people should try without
a doctor gets a note and a link to Find support, which lists eating disorder
helplines for your country. In No numbers, Patterns leaves out the gaps between
meals, because gaps can feed restriction.

### The person who wants every number is welcome too

Being careful about numbers doesn't mean hiding them from people who like them.
All numbers shows calories, macros, vitamins and minerals, and you can set a
deficit or a surplus. Nobody is steered away from the mode they picked.

## What we won't do

- **Show ads or sell your data.** No ad networks and no tracking across other
  apps or sites (`legal/privacy.md`).
- **Shame a day.** No red or orange, no "over", no negative remaining, no
  "you're off track."
- **Grade food.** No food scores, grades, tips after meals or rewards.
- **Project your weight.** No "if every day were like today you'd weigh..."
- **Reward eating less.** Nothing that celebrates reads how much you ate.
- **Push restriction.** A goal Opson works out for you never goes below 1,200
  kcal, and a deficit can't grow past where it started until a dietitian sets
  the limits.
- **Hand someone a goal number they asked not to see.** If you tell onboarding
  that numbers have been hard, it never sets one up for you.
- **Charge for safety.** Every mode, targets, untracked days and Find support
  are free. In the pricing plan's words, safety and personalisation are not
  upsells.
